# Modules

- Dashboard: attachment-defined KPIs, outcomes, WIP/RIP, TAT and staff availability.
- Cases: investigation/death claim lists, assignment, workflow, evidence, notes and timeline.
- Imports: configurable mappings, preview, validation, duplicate protection, commit and rollback.
- Forms/Documents: company/version-specific dynamic forms and DOCX/PDF output.
- Staff/HR: accounts, employee records, online heartbeat, activity, attendance and leave.
- Administration: companies, case types, templates, RBAC, settings, reports and audit.
